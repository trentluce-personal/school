# TCP 1 — External Enablers

**Due: Friday, Sep 18, 11:59 PM** · 15% of the course grade · PowerPoint on Blackboard
One team member uploads. Feedback returns Tuesday, Sep 22.

Goal: describe how the industry is changing over time, and turn that into a list of
insights that will become the new venture idea.

## Required slides

### Consumer and market trends (1–2 slides) — 3G analysis
- Past, present, and future of the industry the business operates or aims to operate in.
  - Specific names for industry moments
  - Evidence to support claims
  - Logos of companies operating at every stage of the industry

### External Environment (1 slide)
- Identification of insights for new venture ideas for **each of the 8 dimensions of
  analysis** (e.g., Economic, Sociocultural, Global, Technological, Political/Legal,
  Ecological, Demographic).

> The syllabus says 8 dimensions but names only 7. Fill the eighth from the lecture deck,
> or ask in the Ask My Instructor forum before the deadline.

## Optional slides
Not required, but they set up TCP 2 and 3 and cost nothing to include.

### Problem (1 slide)
- Description of the problem in a way a layperson will understand.
- Why the problem matters and what people gain from solving it.

### Gap in the marketplace (1 slide)
- Description of the market gap in a way a layperson will understand.
- Why the gap matters and what people gain from filling it.
