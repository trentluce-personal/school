Goal: identify what actually drives purchase decisions in the industry, then map where

**Must include all of TCP 1, refined, with prior feedback addressed.**

## Required slides

### Drivers of consumption (1 slide)
- A coherent and realistic analysis containing all product attributes that drive
  consumption in the target industry (e.g., quality, price).
  - The analysis should converge to **two emotional drivers of consumption** (see PPT).
- Assignment of percentage values for each attribute that drives consumption.

### Competitive landscape (1 slide)
- Data-driven chart showing how each company performs on **both emotional attributes**.
- The sample must be comprehensive:
  - **Direct competitors** — similar products or services
  - **Indirect competitors** — alternative solutions to the same need
  - **Potential entrants** — emerging models or technologies that could reshape the market
- **Sample size matters.** A thin sample distorts results, hides saturation or niche
  opportunity, and reduces analytical credibility. Enough data points to show a realistic,
  evidence-based picture of the field.

### Competitive groups (1 slide — overlay)
- Identification of competitive groups
  - A name for each group
