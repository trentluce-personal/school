# Team Case Project (TCP)

60% of the course grade — four deliverables, 15% each. One five-person team, one
business idea, carried from first analysis to final pitch.

| # | Deliverable | Due | Format | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 00 | Team formation + candidate ideas | week of Aug 24 | Blackboard discussion | not started |
| 01 | [External Enablers](01-external-enablers/) | Fri Sep 18 | `.pptx` | not started |
| 02 | [Competitive Landscape](02-competitive-landscape/) | Fri Oct 9 | `.pptx` + `.xlsx` | not started |
| 03 | [Business Plan](03-business-plan/) | Fri Nov 6 | `.pptx` | not started |
| 04 | [Business Pitch](04-business-pitch/) | Fri Nov 20 | `.mp4` | not started |

One team member uploads by **11:59 PM** on the due date. Feedback returns the following
Tuesday.

## Rules that apply to every TCP

These are the ones that cost points quietly, so they live here rather than being
repeated four times.

- **Cumulative submissions.** Each TCP must contain all prior TCP slides, refined —
  TCP 1 inside TCP 2, TCP 1–2 inside TCP 3, and so on. One continuous venture story.
- **Address prior feedback.** Requirements carry forward. An unresolved issue from
  TCP 1 keeps costing points in TCP 2, 3, and 4.
- **Attachments, not links.** Upload the `.pptx` (and `.xlsx` where required) directly
  to Blackboard. External links are not accepted for grading.
- **Excel behind every chart.** Any competitive landscape or chart must be data-driven
  and verifiable. Submit the spreadsheet that feeds it, and the slide chart must match
  the spreadsheet 1:1 — no redraws.
- **PowerPoint, not Word.** Structure is our own; the instructor provides insights and
  a generic version if needed.

## Resubmits — two for the whole semester
Late work isn't accepted, but any two of the four TCPs may be **resubmitted to improve
a grade**, due the Friday of the following week. The instructor recommends holding one
for TCP 3. Spending both early leaves nothing for the business plan.

## Bonus
+10 points each time the team innovates on the proposed analytical framework. Clear the
idea with the instructor in advance so it still serves the assignment's purpose.

## AI use
Explicitly encouraged — research support, analytical support, creative support, and
presentation support. Framed as an enabler, not a substitute for our own reasoning.
