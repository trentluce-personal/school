# TCP 3 — Business Plan

**Due: Friday, Nov 6, 11:59 PM** · 15% of the course grade · PowerPoint on Blackboard
One team member uploads. Feedback returns Tuesday, Nov 10.

Goal: state the venture's positioning and lay out the full business model.

**Must include all of TCP 1–2, refined, with prior feedback addressed.**

> **This is the one to hold a resubmit for.** The instructor says so directly — it's the
> densest deliverable and the one most worth a second pass.

## Required slides

### Positioning (1 slide — overlay) — Competitive Landscape
- Positioning identified in the competitive landscape.

### Positioning (1 slide — overlay) — 3G analysis
- Overlay demonstrating the current positioning of the business given the market
  trajectory.
  - Including a name or an expression for the positioning

### Business Model (1 slide)
A coherent business model in its 9 dimensions:
1. Key Partners
2. Key Activities
3. Key Resources
4. Value Proposition
5. Customer Relationships
6. Channels
7. Customer Segments
8. Cost Structure
9. Revenue Streams

## Optional slide

### Positioning — Perceptual Map (1 slide)
- Perceptual map illustrating differences in the positioning not thoroughly illustrated
  in previous analyses.

> The syllabus notes that for certain new venture designs a perceptual map is highly
> recommended or even strictly necessary. Decide this deliberately, not by default.
