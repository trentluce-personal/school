# ENTR 310 — The Entrepreneurial Experience

**Semester:** Fall 2026 (Aug 17 – Dec 10) · 3 credits · CRN 12397

**Instructor:** Eric C. Mota — eric.mota@wichita.edu, Woolsey Hall 209Q, 316-978-7584
**Office hours:** Mon & Tue 5:00–7:00 PM, or by appointment
**Prerequisites:** None listed

> The entire course is one five-person team project. 60% of the grade is four
> cumulative team deliverables, 10% is a peer evaluation of my contribution, and 30%
> is a single comprehensive final. **My grade depends on four other people**, which is
> the thing that makes this course different from the rest of the schedule.

## Grade goal — A- or better (tuition reimbursement)

**Need 90% of the weighted total.** A- is 90–92 on the syllabus scale, so the cutoff
is exactly 90. No dropped scores, no extra credit — but two real safety valves exist.

| Category | Weight | Retryable? |
|---|---|---|
| TCP 1–4 | 60% (15% each) | **Yes — 2 resubmits total for the semester** |
| Final exam | 30% | No. One sitting, comprehensive |
| Teamwork (peer eval) | 10% | No. Scored by teammates at semester end |

Where the goal actually gets won or lost:

- **The final is the only unhedged 30%.** With full marks on everything else, a 67% on
  the final still clears 90. With a 90% final, the other 70 points have to average 90%
  too. Either way the final is the single biggest swing and there is no second attempt.
- **Bank the TCPs at 100%.** They're team-produced, resubmittable, and the requirements
  are published in full in the syllabus (see `team-case-project/`). There is no reason
  to lose points here.
- **Spend the resubmits deliberately.** Only two for the whole semester. The instructor
  explicitly recommends saving one for TCP 3 (the business plan, the densest one).
  A resubmit is due the **Friday of the following week**.
- **Teamwork is 10% and peer-scored on a fixed profile scale** — Absent 2, Reactive 6,
  Reactive and constructive 8, Active and constructive 10. Anything below "active and
  constructive" costs 2–8 points off the final grade. Show up to every team meeting and
  be the one proposing things.
- **Bonus exists:** +10 points each time the team innovates on the analytical framework.
  Discuss with the instructor in advance.

## Deductions

TCP grading is by deduction from a published scale, and **it is cumulative** — every
TCP must contain all prior TCP slides, and unresolved feedback from an earlier TCP keeps
costing points in every later one.

| Problem | Points |
|---|---|
| Minor errors | −1 |
| Relevant errors | −3 |
| Need for revision | −5 |
| Missing sections or full revision required | −10 |
