## Course Description

Financial Accounting is the study of accounting as a means of communicating financial information about the activities of business enterprises. ACCT 210 emphasizes the concepts and principles underlying the measurement of income and financial position and examines how financial information is used to evaluate business performance and support economic decision-making.

## Definition of a Credit Hour

Success in this three-credit-hour course is based on the expectation that students will spend, for each unit of credit, a minimum of 45 hours over the length of the semester (normally three hours per unit per week, including instructional time and independent study) for a total of approximately 135 hours devoted to instruction, preparation, studying, and other course-related activities.

## Measurable Student Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

- Perform present value and future value computations.
- Prepare bank reconciliation statements.
- Conduct financial statement ratio analysis.
- Explain and demonstrate the effects of business transactions and economic events on the financial statements.
- Apply generally accepted accounting principles to accounting problems involving:
  - Revenue and expense recognition
  - Deferred and accrued revenues and expenses
  - Accounting for bad debts
  - Cash and bank reconciliations
  - Inventory costing methods, including FIFO, LIFO, and weighted-average
  - Property, plant, and equipment acquisition, depreciation, repair, and disposal
  - Bonds payable, including issuance and retirement
  - Treasury stock transactions
  - Stock dividends and stock splits

## Course Assessments

Course grades are determined through a combination of Modular Activities, Homework, Quizzes, and Examinations. Additional details regarding assignment availability and due dates are provided in the separate Assignment Schedule.

### Modular Activities (MAs)

Each chapter of coverage includes a Modular Activity designed to reinforce the assigned reading and recorded lectures. Modular Activities are completed individually through Cengage CNOW and should generally be completed before beginning the homework assignment. Modular Activities are due Friday evenings at 11:55 p.m. (Central Time).

### Homework Assignments

Homework assignments are completed through Cengage CNOW and provide additional practice applying chapter concepts. Students have three attempts on homework assignments, with the correct answers carrying over and the highest score recorded. Homework assignments are due Saturday evenings at 11:55 p.m. (Central Time).

### Chapter Quizzes

Each instructional module concludes with a chapter quiz designed to assess understanding of the assigned material. Quizzes are completed through Blackboard and should be taken only after completing the reading, lectures, Modular Activity, and Homework. Chapter Quizzes are due Sunday evenings at 11:55 p.m. (Central Time). Chapter quizzes utilize Honorlock for browser security; however, a webcam is not required.

### Examinations

Four examinations are administered throughout the semester. Unlike the weekly assignments, examinations are completed during designated testing windows (24 hours) as published on the Assignment Schedule. (an exception is being made for Exam Four) Each examination is administered through Blackboard using Honorlock. Students are responsible for reviewing the separate Testing Expectations & Honorlock Addendum prior to each examination.
