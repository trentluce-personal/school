# AI policy — MIS 600

Written down because it's easy to forget mid-semester.

**Default: AI is off.** From the syllabus, under Academic Integrity:

> all use of generative AI is prohibited except as may be specified in assignment
> instructions

So it's opt-in per assignment, in writing. Silence means no.

The Week 1 module says so directly, in a section titled "One rule: no AI on this one" —
the reasoning being that dragging the boxes around is what teaches the tool.

**If suspected:** the instructor may require an alternate demonstration of mastery
(verbal interview, extra problem set) before deciding whether to file a report.
Confirmed violations go to the Office of Student Conduct and can mean a failing
grade on the assignment or in the course, under WSU Policy 2.17.

**Practical line for this repo:** using AI to organize files, build study schedules,
or explain a concept from the textbook is a different thing from using it to produce
submitted work. Keep generated content out of anything that gets turned in unless
the assignment says otherwise in writing.

ACCT 210 has the same default-off rule, plus a ban on uploading course assessments
to Chegg, Course Hero, and similar sites.
