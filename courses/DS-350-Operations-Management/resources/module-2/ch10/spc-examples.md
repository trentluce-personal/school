# SPC examples and formula reference

> Readable transcription of [the original examples](SPC%20Examples%20mod_A.html),
> which remain authoritative. Blank working columns are omitted. Image-only
> formulas remain in the original; the numerical factor table is transcribed.

## Example 1: dumbbell weights

A company makes dumbbells for exercising. One kind has a designed weight of
10 ounces. Five samples of four observations (dumbbells) each have been taken
from the assembly line. Use three-sigma control limits. Is the process in
control? Why?

| Sample | Observation 1 | Observation 2 | Observation 3 | Observation 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10.20 | 9.90 | 9.80 | 10.10 |
| 2 | 10.30 | 9.80 | 9.90 | 10.40 |
| 3 | 9.70 | 9.90 | 9.90 | 10.10 |
| 4 | 9.90 | 10.30 | 10.10 | 10.50 |
| 5 | 9.80 | 10.20 | 10.30 | 9.70 |

## Example 3: hotel-suite defects

A hotel has one suite for visiting dignitaries and other VIPs. Housekeeping
associates check the suite immediately after housekeeping and record any
deviation from hotel standards as a defect: a ruffled towel, wilting flowers,
an unstocked bar or refrigerator, etc. Total defects per day are recorded for
15 days. Use three-sigma control limits. Is the process in control? Why?

| Day | Defects |
|---|---|
| 1 | 2 |
| 2 | 0 |
| 3 | 3 |
| 4 | 1 |
| 5 | 2 |
| 6 | 3 |
| 7 | 1 |
| 8 | 0 |
| 9 | 0 |
| 10 | 4 |
| 11 | 2 |
| 12 | 1 |
| 13 | 2 |
| 14 | 3 |
| 15 | 1 |
