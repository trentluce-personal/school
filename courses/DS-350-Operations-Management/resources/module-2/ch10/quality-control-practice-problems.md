## 1. Wiper-blade replacement times

A service center monitors replacement time using mean and range charts. Six
samples, each with n = 20 observations, have these sample means and ranges:

| Sample | Mean | Range |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3.06 | 0.42 |
| 2 | 3.15 | 0.50 |
| 3 | 3.11 | 0.41 |
| 4 | 3.13 | 0.46 |
| 5 | 3.06 | 0.46 |
| 6 | 3.09 | 0.45 |

Using the "Factors for 3-sigma control limits," determine upper and lower limits
for mean and range charts. Is the process in control?
