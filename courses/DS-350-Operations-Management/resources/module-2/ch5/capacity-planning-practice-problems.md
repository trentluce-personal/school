# Strategic capacity planning: practice problems

> Readable transcription of [the original practice problems](D_Strategic%20Capacity%20Planning_AP%281%29.html),
> which remain authoritative. Numbering and spacing are reformatted.

## 1. Utilization and efficiency

Determine utilization and efficiency for each situation.

a. A loan processing operation processes an average of 7 loans per day. Design
capacity is 10 loans per day; effective capacity is 8 loans per day.

b. A furnace repair team services an average of four furnaces per day. Design
capacity is six furnaces per day; effective capacity is five furnaces per day.

c. Will systems with higher efficiency ratios than other systems always have
higher utilization ratios than those other systems? Explain.

## 2. Job shop

Effective capacity is only 50% of design capacity, and actual output is 80% of
effective output. What design capacity is needed to achieve actual output of
eight jobs per week?

## 3. Pottery plant

A pottery producer is considering a new plant to absorb a backlog of demand.
The primary location would have fixed costs of $9,200 per month and variable
costs of $0.70 per unit. Items sell to retailers at an average of $0.90 each.

a. What monthly volume is required to break even?

b. What profit would monthly volumes of 61,000 units and 87,000 units produce?

c. What volume is needed for a profit of $16,000 per month?

d. What volume is needed for revenue of $23,000 per month?

e. Plot total cost and total revenue lines.

## 4. Bottleneck machine

A small firm intends to increase a bottleneck operation's capacity by adding a
machine. Revenue per unit is $15 for either alternative.

| Alternative | Annual fixed cost | Variable cost per unit |
|---|---|---|
| A | $40,000 | $10 |
| B | $30,000 | $11 |

a. Determine each alternative's break-even point in units.

b. At what output volume do the alternatives yield the same profit?

c. If expected annual demand is 12,000 units, which alternative yields higher profit?

## 5. Felt-tip pens

Forecast demand for the coming month is 30,000 pens. The operation has allocated
fixed costs of $25,000 per month and variable costs of $0.37 per pen.

a. Find the break-even quantity if pens sell for $1 each.

b. What selling price produces monthly profit of $15,000, assuming estimated
demand materializes?
