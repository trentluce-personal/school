## Definition of quality

Quality is the ability of a product or service to consistently meet or exceed
customer expectations.

## Quality gurus

- Walter Shewhart: "Father of statistical quality control"
- W. Edwards Deming
- Joseph M. Juran
- Philip B. Crosby
- Kaoru Ishikawa
- Others

## Dimensions of quality: product

| Dimension | Description |
|---|---|
| Performance | Main characteristics of the product/service |
| Aesthetics | Appearance, feel, smell, taste |
| Special features | Extra characteristics |
| Conformance | How well the product/service conforms to customer expectations |
| Reliability | Consistency of performance |
| Durability | Useful life of the product/service |
| Perceived quality | Indirect evaluation of quality, such as reputation |
| Serviceability | Service after sale |

## Dimensions of quality: service

| Dimension | Description |
|---|---|
| Tangibles | Physical appearance of facilities, equipment, employees, etc. |
| Convenience | Availability and accessibility of service |
| Reliability | Ability to perform service dependably, consistently, and accurately |
| Responsiveness | Willingness to help customers in unusual situations |
| Time | Speed with which service is delivered |
| Assurance | Knowledge; building trust and confidence with customers |
| Courtesy | How customers are treated |

## Determinants of quality

- Quality of design: designers' intention to include or exclude features in a
  product or service. The source spells this "Intension."
- Quality of conformance: the degree to which goods or services conform to the
  intent of the designers.
- Ease of use.
- Service.

## Costs of quality

Consequences of poor quality include loss of business, liability, productivity,
and costs.

| Type | Category | Source example |
|---|---|---|
| Costs of poor quality | Internal failure costs | Raw material |
| Costs of poor quality | External failure costs | Warranty |
| Costs of good quality | Appraisal | Inspection |
| Costs of good quality | Prevention | Training |

Quality indexes can be tracked over time.

## Quality awards

- Baldrige: focuses on overall quality, is data driven, and has winners share
  their knowledge.
- Deming: focuses on statistical quality control.
- Others.

## Quality certification

- ISO 9000: international standards on quality management and quality assurance,
  critical to international business.
- ISO 14000: international standards for assessing a company's environmental
  performance.

## Total quality management

A philosophy that involves everyone in an organization in a continual effort to
improve quality and achieve customer satisfaction.

## Six Sigma

"Six Sigma" refers to the plus or minus number of standard deviations from the
mean. The slides describe it as a data driven management philosophy that:

- Reduces defects to 3.4 DPMO.
- Reduces variation in processes.
- Uses tools and techniques.

The Six Sigma team includes top management, program champions, master "black
belts," "black belts," and "green belts."
