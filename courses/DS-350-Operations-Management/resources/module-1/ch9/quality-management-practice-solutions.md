| Customer type | Noisy | Failed | Odor | Warm | Total |

| Residential | 10 | 7 | 5 | 3 | 25 |
| Commercial | 3 | 2 | 7 | 4 | 16 |

The original also contains an alternate check-sheet illustration.

Pareto chart order for each customer type:

| Residential problem | Frequency | Commercial problem | Frequency |

| Noisy | 10 | Odor | 7 |

### Symbol key and combined frequencies

| Symbol | Meaning | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| N/R | Noisy residential customer | 10 |
| N/C | Noisy commercial customer | 3 |
| F/R | Equipment failure, residential customer | 7 |
| F/C | Equipment failure, commercial customer | 2 |
| W/R | Runs warm, residential customer | 3 |
| W/C | Runs warm, commercial customer | 4 |
| O/R | Odor, residential customer | 5 |
| O/C | Odor, commercial customer | 7 |

The combined Pareto chart orders the categories N/R, F/R, O/C, O/R, W/C, N/C,
W/R, F/C. Its axes are "Frequency" and "Problem," with frequency labels 0 to 11.
