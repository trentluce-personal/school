About Forecasts

  • Forecasts are:
    • Rarely perfect because of randomness
    • More accurate for groups vs. individuals
    • Less accurate as time horizon increases
  • Good forecasts are timely, reliable,accurate, meaningful, easy to use,meaningful, etc.
  • Forecasts needs to be continuouslymonitored for accuracy.

Time Series Components

  • Trend-long-term movement in data
  • Seasonality-short-term regularvariations in data
  • Cycle–wavelike variations of more thanone year’s duration
  • Irregular variations-caused by unusualcircumstances
  • Random variations-caused by chance

Time Series (TS) Models

  • Naïve
  • Moving Average (MA)
  • Weighted Moving Average (WMA)
  • Exponential Smoothing (ES)
  • Simple Linear Regression
  • If TS containsRandomvariation use:
    • Moving Average
    • Weighted Moving Average
    • Exponential Smoothing
  • If TS containsRandom+Trenduse:
    • Simple Linear Regression
  • If TS containsRandom+Trend+Seasonalityuse:
    • Simple Linear Regression and Seasonal factors
