## What this course covers

Operations decisions for products and services: quality, capacity, decision theory,
process layout, linear programming, location, forecasting, inventory, MRP/ERP,
project management, and supply chains.

## Study rhythm

- Read the chapters and slides for the **conceptual quizzes**: 15 minutes,
  10 true/false or multiple-choice questions. Quiz 7 has nine cumulative
  multiple-choice questions plus a one-point bonus.
- Use chapter examples, solved problems, instructor voice-over PDFs, homework,
  and recommended practice problems for the **problem-solving exams**. Homework
  alone does not cover the full range of exam problems.
- Homework is untimed and available for at least a week. Begin HW 2 in week 3;
  half covers Ch. 6, before the linear-programming material in week 4.
- Exams are **90 minutes**, open book/open notes, with an instructor-provided
  formula sheet that can be printed. Study and practice before starting.
- Expected course effort is **135 hours total**, roughly 17 hours per week.
